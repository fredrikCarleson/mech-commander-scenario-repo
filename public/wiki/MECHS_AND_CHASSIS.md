# Hangar Chassis Database

In **Meridian Strike**, your tactical options on the battlefield depend on your choice of mech chassis. Chassis range from light 20-ton reconnaissance speedsters to 80-ton assault platforms capable of leveling fortresses.

---

Front and rear armor protect the same core structure. Rear torso hits penetrate
rear armor directly into the core; front armor does not absorb them. Destroyed
arms disable their weapons. One lost leg reduces movement; both lost legs permit
one hex of crawling per activation. Core or Command destruction is fatal.

## Weight Classes Overview

| Class               | Tonnage Range | Speed Range | Role & Capabilities                                                 |
| :------------------ | :------------ | :---------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Light**           | 20 – 25 Tons  | 7 – 8 Hexes | High mobility, scouting, jump jets, flank harass                    |
| **Medium**          | 35 – 40 Tons  | 5 – 6 Hexes | Balanced front-line combat, skirmishing, versatile hardpoints       |
| **Heavy**           | 55 – 60 Tons  | 4 Hexes     | Armored brawling, heavy coil support, missile artillery             |
| **Assault / Siege** | 75 – 80 Tons  | 2 – 3 Hexes | Maximum armor, crushing alpha strikes, long-range siege bombardment |

## Weapon refit

Stock mounts above are the factory loadout. After the first completed main contract, Repair Bay can unlock a **refit facility** (200,000 c). Headquarters **Machines → Loadout** then lets you swap weapons on an owned hull within that chassis’s size and capacity limits. Light frames stay light; assaults can mount heavier racks. Labor costs credits and campaign days. There is no weapon inventory or trade-in — you buy the new system when you commit the job.

## Equipment refit

The same Loadout screen has two equipment slots on chassis below 50 tons and three on chassis at 50 tons or above. Each system is unique per hull, costs credits and workshop time to install, and costs 5,000 c plus one day to remove.

- **Heat Sink:** +2 cooling during Cool and activation-end cooling.
- **Jump Jets:** lets a ground chassis cross chasms and deep water using jump movement. A chassis with integrated jump capability cannot install redundant jets.
- **Sensor Suite:** +3 detection range when a mission uses tactical sensors. It does not reveal or change anything in missions without sensor visibility rules.
- **Armor Plating:** +2 temporary armor on every intact location for each deployment. The extra armor is removed after battle and cannot repair the persistent hull.

Equipment is installed on the owned hull, survives save/load, and is listed with the selected deployment loadout before launch.
