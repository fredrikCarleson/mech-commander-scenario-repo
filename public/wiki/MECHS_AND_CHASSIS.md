# Hangar Chassis Database

In **Meridian Strike**, your tactical options on the battlefield depend on your choice of mech chassis. Chassis range from light 20-ton reconnaissance speedsters to 80-ton assault platforms capable of leveling fortresses.

---

## Weight Classes Overview

| Class               | Tonnage Range | Speed Range | Role & Capabilities                                                 |
| :------------------ | :------------ | :---------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Light**           | 20 – 25 Tons  | 7 – 8 Hexes | High mobility, scouting, jump jets, flank harass                    |
| **Medium**          | 35 – 40 Tons  | 5 – 6 Hexes | Balanced front-line combat, skirmishing, versatile hardpoints       |
| **Heavy**           | 55 – 60 Tons  | 4 Hexes     | Armored brawling, heavy coil support, missile artillery             |
| **Assault / Siege** | 75 – 80 Tons  | 2 – 3 Hexes | Maximum armor, crushing alpha strikes, long-range siege bombardment |
