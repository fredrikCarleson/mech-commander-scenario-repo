# Weapons & Equipment Reference

Weapons in **Meridian Strike** define your engagement distance, heat dissipation management, and target burst potential.

---

## Weapon Trajectories

- **Direct Fire**: Requires clear line of sight (LOS) to target. Blocked by terrain features or high canyon walls unless elevated.
- **Indirect Fire**: Missile systems capable of lobbing ordinance over intervening cover, forest clusters, and hills.

## Combat Mechanics & Range Multipliers

1. **Optimal Range**: Targets standing within optimal range suffer full base damage without accuracy penalties.
2. **Beyond Optimal Range**: Accuracy decreases gradually up to Maximum Range. Firing beyond Maximum Range is illegal.
3. **Cover & Flanking**:
   - Forest / Crater Cover grants +15% defense against direct fire.
   - Rear Arc Attacks bypass forward armor and deal direct damage to the weaker rear torso structure.
