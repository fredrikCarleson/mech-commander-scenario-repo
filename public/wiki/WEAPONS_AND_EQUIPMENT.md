# Weapons & Equipment Reference

Weapons in **Meridian Strike** define your engagement distance, heat dissipation management, and target burst potential.

---

## Weapon Trajectories

- **Direct Fire**: Requires clear line of sight (LOS) to target. Blocked by terrain features or high canyon walls unless elevated.
- **Indirect Fire**: Missile systems capable of lobbing ordinance over intervening cover, forest clusters, and hills.

## Combat Mechanics & Range Multipliers

1. **Optimal Range**: Weapons gain a **+10%** hit bonus in the ideal band (at or inside optimal range, with a short-range grace of 2 hexes). Damage does not change with range.
2. **Too close**: Long guns pay **−10%** hit per hex inside that grace floor. Point-blank weapons (optimal range 2 or less) skip the short-range penalty.
3. **Beyond Optimal Range**: A flat **−15%** long-range hit penalty applies up to Maximum Range. Firing beyond Maximum Range is illegal.
4. **Cover & Flanking**:
   - Forest grants **15%** cover against **direct** fire. Craters grant **10%**. Ruins grant **15%**. Indirect missiles ignore this terrain cover.
   - Rear-arc shots gain **+15%** hit chance. Location rolls favor the torso. Rear torso hits consume rear armor, then shared core structure; front armor does not intervene.
